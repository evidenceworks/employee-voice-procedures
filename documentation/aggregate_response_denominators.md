# Q57 response counts and selected voice items

The unweighted counts in `results/voice_response_denominators.csv` come from the authenticated EWCS 2024 second-edition EU27 response flow. Among 6,988 eligible employees with a recorded Q57 answer, 3,873 answered yes, 1,992 answered no and 1,123 answered “does not know”. Among 6,548 of these employees with complete reports for the selected representation and meeting items, 3,663 answered yes, 1,918 no and 967 “does not know”.

The 440 excluded reports thus comprise 210 yes, 74 no and 156 uncertain Q57 answers. These are unweighted respondent counts, not population prevalence estimates. The three included response counts also agree with the 12 cells in `results/T1_Procedure_by_voice.csv`. See `code/voice_item_response.py` for the arithmetic and `documentation/variable_map.csv` for the variables.
