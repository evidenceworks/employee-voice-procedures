# Sources and rights

The analysis uses *European Working Conditions Survey, 2024*, second edition,
produced by Eurofound and distributed through the UK Data Service as study
9511. Eurofound retains its rights to the original survey materials.

**Source citation:** Eurofound. (2026). *European Working Conditions Survey,
2024* [data collection]. 2nd Edition. UK Data Service. SN: 9511.
https://doi.org/10.5255/UKDA-SN-9511-2

The source data are available to registered researchers under the
[UK Data Service End User Licence](https://ukdataservice.ac.uk/app/uploads/cd137-enduserlicence.pdf)
and any conditions attached to the study. This repository contains published
aggregate statistical outputs, original analysis code and explanatory
materials. It does not include respondent records, the UKDS data archive or
original survey documentation.

The aggregate tables are based on licensed survey data. Public tables retain
disclosure precautions: no respondent identifiers, no country-by-voice-by-outcome
cells and no near-identical omission-model denominators. Blocks are suppressed
where necessary. Their publication does not transfer rights to the underlying data or grant permission to
redistribute source-derived datasets. The source licence and applicable
disclosure requirements remain relevant to further use. Original research
prose and software have separate licenses, as specified in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The software depends on independently distributed Python packages listed in
`requirements.txt`. Those packages retain their respective licenses and are
not bundled here.
